## Design Context

### Users
Tri-Cities owner-operators — contractors, shops, manufacturers, hospitality, and professional offices — who have outgrown spreadsheets, texts, and disconnected tools. They arrive looking for practical systems (quotes, jobs, files, follow-up), not vague “digital transformation.” Context is often a shop floor, job site, tasting-room office, or small ops desk; trust and clarity beat novelty.

### Brand Personality
Practical · Precise · Local. Unflashy confidence: production-ops credibility, transparent offer ladder, demo showroom proof. Voice is direct and operator-facing — no agency fluff.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Industrial Operator Studio** — trustworthy like a local service business, sharp like a product showroom. Warm concrete paper, steel charcoal ink, Columbia river blue accent, sparingly used shop-brass highlight. Light mode only for v1.

**Typography:** Fraunces (display) · Geist (body/UI) · IBM Plex Mono (labels, prices, demo chrome only). Fluid type with `clamp()`; H1 ~2.75rem mobile / ~3.5rem desktop max for hero display.

**Color tokens** (`app/globals.css`): `--ink`, `--paper`, `--surface`, `--line`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--accent-2`, `--danger` (oklch). Accent for links, focus rings, active demo steps, and one hero rule — not full-bleed backgrounds.

**Iconography:** Lucide (stroke 1.5) for UI; monoline industry marks via `components/icons/IndustryMarks.tsx` (hard hat, gear, sign stack, grape, briefcase, wrench) — not emoji, not rounded-full icon pills.

**Motion / assets:** `Reveal` (section fade/slide-up), `DemoCrossfade` (demo step panels), sticky header scroll shadow; `ImagePlaceholder` aspects: hero / industry / about / demo / og.

**Anti-references:** Purple/indigo SaaS gradients; warm cream + terracotta editorial; dark neon/glow tech; generic agency “full-service digital”; Inter/Roboto/system-default as brand type.

### Design Principles
1. **Operator-first clarity** — Every page should answer what the system does and who it’s for without decoration competing for attention.
2. **Proof over abstraction** — UI mockups, demos, and workflow language beat stock handshakes and buzzwords.
3. **Local without keyword stuffing** — Tri-Cities specificity in copy and footer NAP/cities; brand mark `GDG` in chrome, full name in footer.
4. **Scaffold honesty** — No wireframe section labels in the UI; keep optional `data-section` attrs only. Prefer rhythm breaks (lists, bento, image tiles) over equal card grids everywhere.
5. **Accessible by default** — Skip link, `:focus-visible` accent rings, 44px tap targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for Reveal and demo crossfade.
